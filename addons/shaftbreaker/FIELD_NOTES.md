# Field notes

The same journal you fill in game (use the Breaker's Wheel, "Field notes"). Each entry unlocks the first time you witness it; until then only the hint shows.

## 1. It came down after you

About a minute after you go below ground it is down there too. You will not see it arrive. You will hear it: a click, a scrape, breathing that is not yours, and the sounds come from exactly where it is.

*To unlock: Go underground, or stay out after dark, and wait.*

## 2. It does not move

Sometimes it is simply standing there at the edge of your light, perfectly still, turned towards you. It was not there a moment ago.

*To unlock: Look around you more often.*

## 3. Above you

It likes high ledges. From up there it watches you work, and it can drop on you faster than you can climb to it.

*To unlock: Look up from the bottom of a ravine or a tall cave.*

## 4. Upside down

It hangs from cave ceilings head down, arms loose, and turns with you. If dust starts to fall, move out of the way.

*To unlock: Look up in tall chambers.*

## 5. Turn around

You will turn around and it will be right there, a few blocks behind you. It has been there for a while.

*To unlock: Turn around now and then.*

## 6. It knew where you were going

It reads which way you are heading and waits around the next corner. People who keep checking behind them find it ahead of them more often.

*To unlock: Keep walking the same way.*

## 7. Behind the trunk

It hides behind trees and pillars and peeks round them. Look at it and it ducks back. Look away and it peeks out again, from the other side.

*To unlock: Watch the tree trunks at night, and the pillars underground.*

## 8. The standoff

When you spot it, sometimes it stays and tests you: a neck crack, a head tilt, one slow step while you watch.

*To unlock: Let it see that you have seen it.*

## 9. A little closer

Every time you look away it is a little closer. Let it get close enough and it lunges the moment you look back.

*To unlock: Look away from it during a standoff.*

## 10. Do not stare it down

Hold its gaze too long and it snaps. The more you stare at it, the less patience it has for it next time.

*To unlock: What happens if you never look away?*

## 11. Faster than you

When it snaps it screams, the air goes red and it comes faster than you can sprint, climbing walls and pillars to reach you. Nothing can touch you for about two seconds after the scream. Break its line of sight. Daylight ends it, except in its own land.

*To unlock: Make it angry.*

## 12. Do not hit it

Striking it or shooting it makes it snap. It has a great deal of health, and it falls hard when it finally falls. If you mean to fight it, mean it.

*To unlock: Hit it. (Not recommended.)*

## 13. Do not run at it

Rush it and it decides on the spot: it attacks, or it vanishes and comes back when you are weaker. The more often you rush it, the more often it stands its ground.

*To unlock: Run straight at it.*

## 14. It came back

Breathing right behind you, then the coil. It waits for a worse moment: when you are hurt, in the dark, on your own.

*To unlock: Rush it and live.*

## 15. The lunge

A short coil and a growl, then it covers six blocks in a heartbeat. Move sideways on the growl.

*To unlock: Be seen at the wrong moment.*

## 16. Too close

Walk right up to it and it strikes. It does not need a reason.

*To unlock: Walk right up to it.*

## 17. Its face

Sometimes when you turn, it is not a few blocks away. It is in your face.

*To unlock: Turn around at the wrong moment.*

## 18. It vanishes where it stands

It does not walk away. It is simply gone. That does not mean it left.

*To unlock: Watch it leave.*

## 19. Doors are not walls

It opens doors, gates and trapdoors, smashes glass and leaves, and crawls through one-block gaps. Shut yourself in and it goes for the nearest door or window. When it is over, every door is closed and every pane is back, as if it was never there.

*To unlock: Shut a door on it.*

## 20. Do not open the door

At night, if you shut yourself in, it knocks. Then it waits on the other side of the door.

*To unlock: Stay inside at night.*

## 21. Your own sounds

A door you did not open. Footsteps that stop when you stop. Someone eating in the dark. Your own pickaxe in another tunnel. It has been listening to you.

*To unlock: Listen while you work.*

## 22. Whispers

Whispers just behind your head. Sometimes words on your screen. Sometimes your name.

*To unlock: Spend time near it.*

## 23. Two pale points

Eyes in the dark, a face-width apart. They go out the moment you look straight at them.

*To unlock: Look into the dark you are not lighting.*

## 24. Its dens

Chambers carpeted in grey dust and bone, the walls scored by its claws, spines hanging from the ceiling. Break the Den Heart and the territory goes quiet for a few days. It remembers who did that.

*To unlock: Follow a shaft moth.*

## 25. Shaft moths

A pale moth that leads you to its den, and waits and points when you fall behind.

*To unlock: Stay underground near a den.*

## 26. The Hollows

Its own land: grey dust, dead timber, old headframes over shafts that drop into the dark, cairns nobody living built. The fog never lifts, and here it walks by day.

*To unlock: Find its land.*

## 27. It can be brought down

Bring it down and it collapses. Its skull is yours to wear. It will come back.

*To unlock: Survive the unsurvivable.*
